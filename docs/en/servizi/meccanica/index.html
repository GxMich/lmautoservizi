Frequently asked questions

Do you service all makes of car?

Yes, I work on cars, 4x4s, vans and campervans of all makes. Send me the make, model and mileage and I'll prepare a quote.

Can you service my car while it's still under warranty?

Yes: in general, under EU rules, the manufacturer's warranty stays valid as long as you follow its maintenance schedule. Warranty repairs and recalls, however, stay with the official dealer network. If in doubt, bring me the service book and we'll look at it together.

I hear a noise when I brake: is it serious?

A noise when braking should be checked soon, rather than left. Bring it in: I'll look at it on the lift and tell you what's needed and what it costs.

What if you find something else during the job?

I call you and explain what I've found. I only do extra work once you've given me the OK.

How long does the car stay in for a service?

It depends on the vehicle and what the service includes. When we arrange it, I tell you when to bring it in and when to collect it. As I'm open all day, you can drop it off before work and pick it up afterwards.
