Frequently asked questions

When should winter tyres go on?

Where ordinances require it, you need winter tyres or chains on board, usually from 15 November to 15 April. Winter tyres can go on from 15 October. Summer tyres go back on by 15 May.

How often should wheel alignment be done?

It depends on the car and how you use it. It's worth checking when you fit new tyres or after a hard knock against a pothole or kerb. Also if the car pulls to one side or the tyres wear unevenly.

Can a puncture be repaired, or do I need a new tyre?

It depends on where the hole is and how big it is. I'll look at it and tell you if it can be safely repaired or needs replacing.

Do you sell tyres too?

Yes, summer, winter and all-season. Send me the size printed on the tyre sidewall, for example 205/55 R16, or the number plate. I'll reply with availability and price.

Do you do balancing and alignment too?

Yes, the workshop has the equipment for fitting, balancing and alignment. If the alignment needs doing, I tell you before I do it.
