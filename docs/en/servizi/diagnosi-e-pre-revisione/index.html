When is your test due?

The first roadworthiness test (revisione) is due within four years of first registration, then every two years.

The deadline is the end of the month: a car first registered in March 2023 is due by March 2027. After that, by the same month as the last test, two years later.

This applies to cars and vehicles up to 3.5 tonnes: heavier ones, such as some motorhomes, are tested every year.

Frequently asked questions

The engine warning light is on: can I keep driving?

It depends on the light and how the car behaves. If it's red or flashing, or the car behaves differently, stop when it's safe and call me. Otherwise, have the fault code read soon.

Do you carry out the roadworthiness test (revisione)?

The test is carried out only by the Motorizzazione or authorised centres. I check the vehicle for free beforehand, tell you if anything needs fixing and book the test for you.

How often is the roadworthiness test due?

The first test is due within four years of first registration, then every two years. Tell me when yours is due and I'll arrange the free check beforehand.

What do you check in the pre-inspection?

I look at the points checked during the test, such as lights, brakes, tyres and warning lights. If I find anything, I tell you what's needed and what it costs, and you decide.

I've been told my ECU needs programming: can you do it?

I program ECUs with software updated every day. Send me the make, model and year: I'll tell you if I can do it and what it costs.

Can the diagnosis be done away from the workshop?

I usually do it in the workshop. In simple cases, the mobile workshop lets me read warning lights on site, in Casale Monferrato and nearby villages.
